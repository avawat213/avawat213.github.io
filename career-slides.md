# Career Overview
- Licensed mental health therapists support individuals, couples, families, and groups.
- They assess needs, create treatment plans, and teach coping strategies.
- Work settings include private practice, clinics, hospitals, schools, and telehealth.

---

# Key Skills and Training
- Empathy and active listening
- Communication and ethical judgment
- Critical thinking and cultural awareness
- Master’s degree plus state licensure
- Supervised clinical hours and continuing education

---

# AI Disruption
- AI chatbots and apps can offer 24/7 support and initial screening.
- Digital tools can automate notes, scheduling, and billing.
- Therapists may focus more on complex emotional work.
- Technology requires therapists to monitor fairness, privacy, and consent.

---

# AI Benefits
- Less paperwork means more time with clients.
- AI can help identify patterns and track progress.
- Clients can access guided tools between sessions.
- Telehealth becomes more efficient with AI scheduling and summaries.

---

# How to Gain an Advantage
- Use AI to summarize research and compare therapy methods.
- Practice mock therapy sessions with AI clients.
- Learn digital mental health tools and ethical guidelines.
- Keep organized with AI study planners and reflection journals.

---

# Action Plan
- Take an introductory psychology or counseling course.
- Practice weekly AI-based case simulations.
- Research AI mental health platforms monthly.
- Volunteer or practice peer counseling.
- Study privacy, ethics, and digital confidentiality.

---

# Main Takeaway
- AI will support therapists, not replace them.
- Human empathy and trust remain essential.
- Therapists who use AI responsibly will be more effective.
- Preparing now will help future success in this field.
